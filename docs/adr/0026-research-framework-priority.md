# ADR 0026: research-framework priority and proportionate evidence

- **Status:** Superseded by ADR 0027
- **Date:** 2026-07-20

## Context

The seven-market data foundation is sufficient to begin research-framework work, but capture release
qualification accumulated production-style compatibility and evidence machinery while strategy
evaluation remained prohibited. q-trad is an experimental single-user project whose continuation
depends on obtaining trustworthy strategy evidence.

## Decision

Make the shortest trustworthy path to reproducible, cost-aware strategy evaluation the current
priority. Admit a reviewed approximately 20-market research universe, multiple shadow paper
strategies, causal outcome evaluation, basic effectiveness ranking and diagnostic market state.

Retain correctness that protects research conclusions: causal timing, executable-side fills, costs,
product economics, visible data loss, deterministic replay and independent accounting. Keep the
running collector immutable except through its existing reviewed operational boundary.

Before a decision-grade experiment cites an epoch, internal schemas and APIs may change
incompatibly. Prefer a bounded migration, re-export or rebuild over indefinite dual compatibility.
Archive completed chronology outside the normal documentation reading path.

## Consequences

Unfinished storage studies, exhaustive capture qualification, broad rollback compatibility and
operator tooling no longer block the research-framework proof unless a measured risk makes them
necessary. External orders, automatic real-capital promotion and live execution remain excluded.
Negative strategy evidence is a successful project outcome and may justify stopping further work.
